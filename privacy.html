Privacy Policy for PIE Tracker

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data storage

PIE Tracker (Personal Income Expense Tracker) stores your transactions, accounts, categories, currencies and settings in your browser's local storage on your own device.

If you choose to connect Google Drive sync, PIE Tracker uses Google OAuth solely to keep a copy of that data in a single file (pie-sync.json) in your own Google Drive. It uses the drive.file permission, which only allows access to files PIE Tracker itself created.

2. Data collection and usage

PIE Tracker does not own, operate or maintain any external database or server to store, transmit or process your personal information. All app data stays on your device and, if you connect it, in your own Google account.

3. Screen privacy

Screen privacy is optional and off by default. If you turn it on:

  • Your code is never stored. Only a one-way verifier (PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random salt) is saved, and synced to your own Drive file so the same code works on your other devices.
  • The recovery code is stored the same way.
  • The recovery email is stored only as a SHA-256 fingerprint, not as the address.
  • If you use Verify with Google to reset a forgotten code, PIE Tracker asks Google for your email address (openid email), compares it with the stored fingerprint on your device, and then discards it. The address is not stored or sent anywhere else.

Screen privacy hides screens; it does not encrypt your financial data.

4. Third-party sharing

We do not collect, share, track or sell any user data or personal information to third parties.

5. Access revocation

You can revoke PIE Tracker's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions settings, or disconnect from Settings → Data → Google Sync in the app.
