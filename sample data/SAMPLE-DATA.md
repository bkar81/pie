# Sample data for testing PIE Tracker

`sample-data.json` is a small, entirely fake dataset you can load to try PIE Tracker before entering your own income and expenses. Every merchant name, amount and note is made up.

**What's in it**

- **Categories in all three groups**
  - Expense: Food, Grocery, Personal, Apparel, Household, Recharge, Books/Stationery, Fancy Store, Health, Transportation, Gift, Other
  - Income: Salary, Freelance, Interest, Rental
  - Both: Refund
- **Wallet**
  - Expense accounts: Credit Card (HDFC Regalia, ICICI Amazon Pay — with payment/settlement on), Debit Card, UPI (GPay, PhonePe), Bank Transfer, Cash
  - Income accounts: Employer (Acme Corp), Bank Account (SBI Savings, HDFC Savings), Online (PayPal)
- **Currencies:** INR (default), USD and SGD
- **184 transactions from 1 Aug to 24 Oct 2026**
  - Monthly salary, flat rent received, a festival bonus, quarterly interest and a refund
  - Freelance income paid in USD
  - A four-day Singapore trip in September, paid in SGD
  - Daily food, cabs, weekly groceries, monthly recharges and subscriptions
  - Card charges up to September marked paid; October charges still pending

Open **Analysis → Month → Both** for October 2026 to see everything at once, and the **Calendar** for September to spot the ★ trip days.

## How to load it

1. Open PIE Tracker.
2. Go to **Settings → Data**.
3. If you already have real data, back it up first (**Complete backup → Backup everything**) so you can restore it afterwards.
4. Under **Complete backup**, tap **Restore everything** and choose `sample-data.json`.
5. When you're done exploring, restore your own backup the same way, or use **Reset all data** to start fresh.

Restoring sample data never changes your screen-privacy settings or this phone's default type and currency.
